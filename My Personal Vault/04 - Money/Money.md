# Money

Budgets and goals only. **Never write an account number, card number, login, PIN or password in this folder or anywhere in the vault.** If a note needs to point at an account, say which account in plain words ("the checking account at my main bank") and where the details are kept (for example "in my password manager"). The assistant will not copy account or card numbers into a note, even if you paste them in chat; it will ask you to leave them out.

What belongs here: the monthly budget, savings goals, a list of regular bills (name, rough amount, due day), plans for big purchases, and notes on money decisions you have made. Official answers you want kept fixed (for example "the rent is due on the 1st") go in [[Settled-Facts]]. Money notes are private: the assistant keeps them in the vault and does not copy them into a message without your yes for that one message.

**Keeping this list current:** whenever a note in this folder is created, renamed, moved or changed in a meaningful way, the assistant updates the list below (one line per note) in the same pass. When a note moves to another folder, it comes off this list and goes onto that folder's list.
