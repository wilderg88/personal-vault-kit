# Weekly review

**The job:** once a week (pick a day that suits you, such as Sunday evening), go through what is open, what is loose and what is finished, and leave the vault true and tidy. This is also the worked example of a Job note: copy its shape for your own jobs.

## Boot chain (read these, in order, and you have the skill)
1. This note, end to end.
2. [[Active Priorities]]: the one list of open work.
3. [[Inbox]]: anything dropped and not yet sorted.
4. The last seven daily notes in [[Daily Notes]]: what happened, what was decided.
5. [[Goals and Projects]]: so progress on goals is checked, not guessed.

Do not load the whole vault. Open other notes only when an item points to them.

## The procedure
1. Check today's real date and name the week being reviewed.
2. Read the last seven daily notes. For anything a future session would need that is only in a daily note, save it to its proper note and folder index now (a daily note is a log, never the record).
3. Open items: go through [[Active Priorities]] line by line. For each, check the evidence (the actual note or file) before calling it done. Tick finished items and move them to Completed; flag anything stuck and say what is blocking it.
4. Inbox: sort each item into its proper folder and that folder's index, then remove it from the Inbox. Ask me when you cannot tell where something belongs.
5. Archive: list notes that are finished or replaced. Propose moving them to `08 - Archive`; move them only after my yes, and update both folder indexes.
6. Goals: for each goal in [[Goals and Projects]], note one line of progress this week, or say there was none.
7. Coming week: list appointments and deadlines found in the notes, and suggest the top three priorities.
8. Write the result into today's daily note under a heading "Weekly review", then read it back to confirm it saved.

## Quality bar
- Every "done" claim is backed by evidence you checked, not a guess.
- No index is out of date: every note created, moved or archived is listed in its folder index.
- Nothing is deleted. Nothing is sent, posted, bought or booked without my yes.
- The summary I see is short: what closed, what is stuck, what you propose, and the one question you need answered (ask one thing, then stop).
