# Routines and Jobs

Your routines and the Job notes. A Job note is one note per recurring task (a weekly review of the budget, a monthly home check, a trip packing list): it tells the assistant exactly which notes to read and how to do the task your way. The full list of Jobs is in [[Jobs]].

**Keeping this list current:** whenever a note in this folder is created, renamed, moved or changed in a meaningful way, the assistant updates the list below (one line per note) in the same pass. When a note moves to another folder, it comes off this list and goes onto that folder's list.

## Notes in this folder
- [[Jobs]]: the list of Job notes
- [[Job template]]: blank template for a new Job
- [[Weekly review]]: the weekly check of open items, the inbox and what to archive
