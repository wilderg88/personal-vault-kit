# Goals and Projects

The things you are working toward: a trip, a move, a course, a big clean-out, a savings target. One note per goal or project, with the plan, the next step and the status. What is open this week is also listed in [[Active Priorities]]; the goal note holds the detail. Firm decisions (dates, budgets you have locked) live in [[Settled-Facts]] and goal notes point there instead of repeating them.

**Example with made-up data (delete once real notes are added):** a note called `Spring Garden.md` with: goal, "Plant a vegetable bed by 1 May"; next step, "Buy soil and seeds"; status, "planning"; and a link to the budget line in [[Money]].

**Keeping this list current:** whenever a note in this folder is created, renamed, moved or changed in a meaningful way, the assistant updates the list below (one line per note) in the same pass. When a note moves to another folder, it comes off this list and goes onto that folder's list.
