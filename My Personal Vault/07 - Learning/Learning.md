# Learning

Things you are learning or want to learn: courses, books, languages, skills, hobbies and reading lists. One note per subject, holding your goal, where you are, what comes next and the short notes worth keeping. A subject you practise on a schedule (for example a weekly language session) can get a Job note in `09 - Routines and Jobs`, and this folder links to it.

Keep summaries and your own takeaways here, not copies of whole books or paid course material. Never put course logins or passwords here; say where they are kept instead (for example "in my password manager").

**Keeping this list current:** whenever a note in this folder is created, renamed, moved or changed in a meaningful way, the assistant updates the list below (one line per note) in the same pass. When a note moves to another folder, it comes off this list and goes onto that folder's list.
